## Design Context

### Users
Primary visitors are hiring managers reviewing a frontend developer portfolio. They should be able to open a small number of desktop folders and quickly understand the developer's role, technical judgment, and project outcomes.

### Brand Personality
Quiet, capable, atmospheric. The experience should feel like a personal workstation in orbit: distinctive enough to be memorable, but controlled enough that the work remains the focus.

### Aesthetic Direction
A dark desktop OS floating over a Three.js space field. It is an original desktop metaphor, not a Windows 11 recreation: subdued chrome, soft purple accents, precise typography, and minimal translucent surfaces. The desktop folders are the primary navigation; each window is a focused reading surface.

### Design Principles
1. Let project evidence lead; visual effects support orientation and never compete with content.
2. Keep the desktop metaphor functional: folders launch content, the taskbar reflects open work, and window state is always clear.
3. Use darkness, depth, and restrained soft-purple accents instead of neon or broad gradients.
4. Prefer fewer, stronger surfaces over decorative glass panels and fake controls.
5. Preserve keyboard access, readable Korean copy, and reduced-motion-friendly behavior.
